# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers support delivery health by aligning engineering capacity, technical planning, and dependency management with project goals.

### Responsibilities
- Own delivery planning at the engineering team level
- Coordinate sprint capacity, resourcing, and workload balancing
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers on sequencing and trade-offs
- Support developers with technical planning, blockers, and escalation paths
- Monitor team health, staffing risks, and cross-team dependencies

### Goals
- Maintain predictable delivery velocity and team focus
- Reduce friction across engineering and project planning
- Ensure technical work is feasible, prioritized, and sustainable

### Typical Communication
- Weekly engineering planning sessions
- Sprint reviews and dependency check-ins
- Escalations with PM and Product leads when delivery constraints arise

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works closely with Developers to unblock execution and improve delivery quality
- Aligns with Product Managers on roadmap priorities and trade-offs
- Coordinates with Project Managers to keep milestones, risks, and resource plans realistic

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
The Security / Compliance Lead ensures that projects follow secure-by-default practices and meet required controls, privacy, and governance expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and control expectations for the project
- Review architecture, dependencies, and workflows for risk and compliance impact
- Partner with engineering teams on remediation planning and risk acceptance
- Support audits, required sign-offs, and policy alignment
- Identify security-related dependencies or escalations early in planning

### Goals
- Reduce preventable security and compliance risk
- Increase confidence in release readiness and control coverage
- Support delivery without creating unnecessary friction for product teams

### Typical Communication
- Design review checkpoints and security-related planning meetings
- Risk briefings for product, PM, and leadership stakeholders
- Escalations via project risk and incident communication channels

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Developers to review implementation risks and control requirements
- Partners with Product Managers and Project Managers to ensure security work is included in scope and timelines
- Coordinates with stakeholders on compliance obligations and release gating when needed

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
The QA Lead defines quality standards, validation strategy, and release readiness expectations to ensure the product meets acceptance criteria and customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Define the overall test strategy for features, regressions, and releases
- Establish quality gates and acceptance validation criteria
- Coordinate test planning, automation priorities, and defect triage
- Partner with Product Managers and Developers on readiness and risk assessment
- Support release sign-off and quality reporting

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, confidence-building validation for every milestone
- Reduce defects that affect customer trust or release quality
- Balance speed with a disciplined quality process

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and release readiness checkpoints
- Daily or sprint-level issue triage and validation reviews
- Stakeholder updates on quality status and release risk

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Developers to define testability and regression coverage
- Collaborates with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and release criteria
- Supports Project Managers with risk tracking and release gating decisions

---

## Release Manager / Deployment Lead

### Role Summary
The Release Manager / Deployment Lead coordinates rollout timing, deployment readiness, rollback planning, and stakeholder communication for project releases.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release plans, deployment windows, and rollback readiness
- Ensure staging validation and smoke tests are complete before production
- Partner with engineering, QA, and support teams on release execution
- Communicate release status, known issues, and operational impacts
- Coordinate post-release verification and incident response if needed

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and deployment surprises
- Improve communication and readiness across teams
- Support smooth production adoption of changes

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment checklists
- Stakeholder updates before, during, and after release
- Incident or rollback coordination during critical release issues

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Aligns with Developers and QA Lead on validation readiness and deployment gates
- Partners with Project Managers on milestone and announcement timing
- Works with Stakeholders and Customer Success teams to communicate customer impact and support needs

---

## Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
The Customer Success / Stakeholder Liaison connects delivery work to customer impact, adoption, and business alignment so stakeholders stay informed and engaged.

### Responsibilities
- Gather customer and stakeholder feedback on product experience and impact
- Translate customer concerns into product and delivery priorities
- Coordinate stakeholder updates, change communication, and adoption readiness
- Support launch messaging, training needs, and post-release follow-up
- Surface risks to product and project teams when customer impact is at stake

### Goals
- Improve customer trust and adoption of delivered value
- Keep stakeholders informed and aligned with product outcomes
- Ensure project delivery is connected to real business and user impact

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or milestone-based stakeholder updates
- Customer feedback sessions and adoption check-ins
- Release impact and support coordination with internal teams

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Product Managers to validate customer value and priorities
- Turns project status and release updates into clear business communication for stakeholders
- Coordinates with Project Managers and Release Manager to support smooth rollout and readiness

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The combined persona model helps clarify accountability, handoffs, and escalation paths across product, delivery, quality, security, and stakeholder work.
